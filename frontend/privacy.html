Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Because FreePythonCompiler operates primarily in your browser natively and securely, we do NOT collect, store, or transmit your source code, projects, or keystrokes to any remote servers. Your intellectual property remains exclusively on your machine.

2. Local Storage

We may use your browser's local storage (e.g. localStorage or Virtual File System APIs) strictly to save application preferences such as your choice of Light or Dark mode, UI panel sizes, and your local project files for persistence between refreshes. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-party Analytics

We do not use aggressive third-party trackers. Basic web server logs (which capture IP addresses for security and load balancing purposes) are maintained temporarily by our hosting providers to prevent abuse.

4. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Changes will be reflected directly on this page instantly. We recommend checking back periodically to stay informed.

Privacy FAQ

Frequently Asked Privacy Questions

Transparent explanations of how our client-side architecture safeguards developer privacy.

Does FreePythonCompiler log, store, or inspect my source code?

Direct Answer: No. FreePythonCompiler runs all code locally inside your browser via WebAssembly. Your source code and execution output never leave your computer or touch our servers.

What personal information is collected when using the compiler?

Direct Answer: We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal profiles. We only collect standard anonymous aggregated analytics (such as page views and browser types) to ensure site reliability.

Is FreePythonCompiler compliant with GDPR and CCPA?

Direct Answer: Yes. Because we practice data minimization and do not store personally identifiable developer data or source code, we fully comply with global data protection regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

Are local workspace files stored securely?

Direct Answer: Your local virtual files and project workspace are stored strictly inside your browser's local IndexedDB sandbox, isolated from other websites and external servers.

How can I exercise my privacy rights or request data removal?

Direct Answer: You can reach out to our data privacy team at privacy@freepythoncompiler.com or submit a request via our Contact page.
