Is there an official Thonny online compiler?

Direct Answer: The official Thonny IDE is designed as a desktop application. FreePythonCompiler offers the premier online browser alternative, replicating Thonny's simplicity and student focus without requiring downloads or installation.

Can I run Python on school Chromebooks without administrator access?

Direct Answer: Yes. Because FreePythonCompiler executes purely inside your web browser via WebAssembly (Pyodide), no installer or admin credentials are required. Simply open the URL and begin coding.
