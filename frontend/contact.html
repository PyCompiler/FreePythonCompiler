Frequently Asked Support & Community Questions

Quick answers to common questions about communication, bug reports, and partnerships.

What is the typical response time for support inquiries?

Direct Answer: Our engineering team typically responds to all developer inquiries, bug reports, and partnership requests within 24 business hours.

How can I submit a feature request or report an editor bug?

Direct Answer: You can submit feature suggestions and bug reports directly through the contact form on this page by selecting 'Bug Report' or 'Feature Request' in the inquiry topic dropdown.

Can our school or organization integrate FreePythonCompiler into our curriculum?

Direct Answer: Yes. We actively assist educators with curriculum integration, custom embed configurations, and project templates. Reach out via the form with your institutional details.

How do I discuss sponsorship or advertising opportunities?

Direct Answer: For brand sponsorships, developer tool promotions, or institutional partnerships, select 'Partnership / Sponsorship' in the contact form or visit our dedicated Advertise page.

Do you provide one-on-one debugging for personal homework assignments?

Direct Answer: While our engineering support focuses on platform functionality, compiler bugs, and feature requests, we maintain extensive guides and tutorials on our Blog to help students solve common programming challenges.
